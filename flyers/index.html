How to print these

  1. Open the flyer in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or ⌘ + P (Mac). Choose "Save as PDF".
  3. In the print dialog: Margins → None. Background graphics → ON (or "More settings"). Scale → Default.
  4. Take the saved PDF to any print shop. Tell them: "A4 colour, single-sided, 130–170gsm paper" for posters; "adhesive label paper" for the sticker sheet.
  5. For longevity outdoors: ask for lamination (matt finish, A4 lamination is cheap in Ghana).

Brand & content lock

  1. Colours: navy #1B2A4A, gold #F5A800. Don't replace.
