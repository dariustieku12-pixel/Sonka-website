Print spec

How to print these

  1. Open the flyer in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or ⌘ + P (Mac). Choose "Save as PDF".
  3. In the print dialog: Margins → None. Background graphics → ON. Scale → Default.
  4. Take the saved PDF to any print shop. For posters: A4 colour, 130–170gsm. For stickers: adhesive label paper. For outdoor use: add matt lamination.

Brand & content lock

  1. Colours: navy #1B2A4A, gold #F5A800. Never replace.
  2. Every flyer carries the SONKA LOGISTICS brand bar, one QR (web app, auto-routes to right store per device), and both WhatsApp numbers 0538 214 744 · 0545 989 494.
