Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short. SONKA is free for customers. Drivers pay a monthly subscription and anyone can pay to advertise. Nothing is ever charged automatically. If we charge you twice, take money and don't deliver, or make a mistake, we pay you back.

1. What you can pay for

  • Driver subscription: after your free rides, a monthly fee that depends on your vehicle, from GHS 15 to GHS 25 a month. The exact price for your vehicle is shown before you pay.
  • Adverts (boosts): a fixed price for the number of days you choose, shown before you pay.

Payments are made by mobile money (or card through Paystack). There are no hidden fees, no automatic renewals and no charges through Google Play or the App Store. Finding drivers, calling them and booking is free.

2. When we refund

What happenedWhat we do
You were charged twice for the same thingRefund the extra payment in full
You paid but your subscription or advert was not switched on within 24 hoursSwitch it on straight away, or refund in full if you prefer
We reject your advert before it goes liveRefund in full
We stop a running advert because of a SONKA mistakeRefund the unused days
SONKA is down for more than 3 days in a row because of a fault on our sideAdd the lost days to your subscription

3. When we do not refund

  • A subscription month that has already started, if you simply change your mind. It runs to the end of the month and then stops. Nothing renews automatically.
  • An advert you ask us to stop early. The unused days become SONKA credit for a future advert, not cash.
  • An advert we take down for breaking the advertising rules you accepted before paying, for example scams, sexual content, political campaigning, or using someone's picture without their consent.
  • Accounts suspended for fraud or for breaking our Terms of Service.

Payments for trips are agreed directly between customers and drivers and do not pass through SONKA, so SONKA cannot refund them. If something goes wrong with a trip, use Report a problem in the app and we will help where we can.

4. How to ask for a refund

  • Within 30 days of paying, contact Help & Support in the app, or email support@sonkagh.com.
  • Give the phone number on your SONKA account and the mobile money transaction ID.
  • We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds are sent to the same mobile money number that paid, within 7 working days.

5. Your other rights

This policy does not take away any rights you have under Ghanaian law.
