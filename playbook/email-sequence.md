## EMAIL 3 — Day 4

**Subject:** The pattern that made AI actually build my app
**Preheader:** 4 steps. Changed everything.

Hey {{first_name | "there"}},

Most people fail with AI coding tools because they ask:

> *"Build me an Uber."*

That's like walking into a kitchen and saying *"make food"*. You'll get something. But not what you wanted.

The pattern that actually worked for me — and is the heart of Module 4 in the playbook:

1. **Start with the OUTCOME**, not the feature.
   *Bad:* "Build a map screen."
   *Good:* "I want a customer to open the app and see drivers near them within 5 seconds."

2. **Ask for a PLAN before any code.**
   *"Walk me through how you'd architect this, in plain English, before writing anything."*

3. **Approve the plan back in plain English.**
   *"Yes do that, but use OpenStreetMap not Mapbox, and skip the rating feature for now."*

4. **Then say "go" — and READ the explanation, not just the code.**
   When something looks wrong, ask *"why did you do it this way?"* — the answer teaches you.

This 4-step loop is what separates ship-it-this-month builders from try-it-and-give-up tourists.

Try it on a small idea you have today. Even just opening Claude Code and going through these 4 steps for one feature.

— Darius
